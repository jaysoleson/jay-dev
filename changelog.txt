<strong>Pridegen: Remastered!</strong>
Pridegen has received a complete makeover. The code has been rebuilt from scratch with modern Clangen as a base.
Here's a rundown of the most noticeable changes to players.

<strong>Changes</strong>
-   <strong>UI</strong>: A brand new "Change Sexuality" screen has replaced the old spiderweb of buttons and windows in the profile screen. It provides a new, convenient place to change every cat's acespec, arospec, and gender attraction, along with custom labels.
-   <strong>Profile Display</strong>: Identities listed in a cat's profile are now more concise and readable. Rather than labels being stacked on different lines, they're combined into one sentence.
-   <strong>New Settings</strong>: The new "xenogenders and microlabels" toggle, which is off by default, will allow microlabels and xenogenders to be randomly attainable by cats in game.
-   <strong>Relationships</strong>: Cats' relationships will begin to reflect their sexuality before they realise it! For example, a male cat may begin to lose romantic interest in she-cats a few moons before coming out as gay.
-   <strong>Content</strong>: More coming out events, pride-related dialogue, and pride flag accessories.

On top of this, a lot of invisible changes have been made to the code. It's a lot better now, which means I'll have an easier time adding onto it in the future.

Since this version of Pridegen was rebuilt from scratch, it's possible some stuff may have been forgotten. If you notice an old feature is missing, let me know and I may be able to add it back in!
